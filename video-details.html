Masterclass Summary

In this 14-minute 4K macro masterclass, our senior horologist disassembles a modern automatic caliber. We observe the kinetic path of energy as the weighted rotor rotates on miniature zirconium oxide ball bearings, through the reverser wheels, into the Nivaflex mainspring barrel, and out through the Swiss lever escapement.

Full Masterclass Transcript

[00:00] Welcome to AURELIS Horological Masterclasses. Today we are conducting a complete teardown of a high-grade Swiss self-winding automatic caliber.

[02:15] Here we see the central oscillating weight. Notice the dense outer tungsten rim designed to maximize the moment of inertia while maintaining low overall height.

[05:40] Now examining the mainspring barrel. Notice the internal slipping bridle—this prevents the spring from over-winding when continuous kinetic wrist movement is applied.

[08:50] Inspecting the escapement. Here the ruby pallet fork stones lock and unlock against the escape wheel at 8 beats per second (28,800 vibrations per hour).

[11:30] Look at the hairspring breathing concentricity. The overcoil curve ensures equal expansion in all six spatial positions.

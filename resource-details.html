Pre-Owned & Vintage Watch Due Diligence Checklist

An 18-point verification framework used by senior appraisers and collectors to assess authenticity, case recasting, movement rate amplitude, and dial integrity.

Overview & Purpose

Navigating the secondary and vintage luxury watch market requires rigorous mechanical and cosmetic verification. This standardized inspection rubric eliminates subjective guesswork, providing clear pass/fail thresholds across four critical categories:

What This Document Contains:

  • Case & Bevel Geometry: Identifying over-polished lugs, laser-welded recasting, softened chamfers, and original satin brush lines.
  • Dial & Lume Authenticity: Verifying period-correct fonts, pad printing relief, tritium vs Super-LumiNova decay patina, and original hands.
  • Timegrapher Calibration Rubric: Healthy rate deviations ($\pm4$ to $\pm8$ s/day), balance amplitude thresholds ($>260^\circ$ at full wind), and maximum acceptable beat error ($<0.5$ ms).
  • Provenance & Serial Verification: Cross-matching reference numbers between lugs, inner caseback stamps, movement caliber serials, and archival extracts.

Who This Is For:

Serious Collectors

Use during private acquisitions, auction previews, and dealer negotiations.

Estate Appraisers

Standardize valuation reporting for insurance replacement and legal provenance archives.
